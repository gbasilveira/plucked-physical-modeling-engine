### What to change

1. **Stopping rule.** Replace `|f| ≤ 10⁻¹⁵ |a|` by a test against the residual's natural magnitude: stop when |f| ≤ 8 ε (|a| + |c| (|S⁺| + |S|)), ε = 2⁻⁵² (the round-off floor of the expression that computes f), or when the iteration stagnates: |Δa| ≤ 4 ε |a| on two consecutive iterations with |f| not decreasing. Keep the iteration cap (50) and the `RefError` when neither holds; the error message gives the step, |f|, the floor and the last |Δa|. Record the stopping reason counts and the largest |f| / floor ratio in `Results::newton` (new fields; existing fields unchanged) and in the feature JSON.
2. **No silent change elsewhere.** Every existing P0.5 test and scenario result changes by at most 10⁻¹² relative (stopping one iteration earlier or later at the floor moves a by a few ulps); state the largest change you observe on the existing scenarios.
3. **Documentation (docs/dev/reference-solver.md, section on R6):** the new stopping rule and why; the convergence order of the front-arrival time measured on a ladder (at least four grids), with the per-step error ratios; your analysis of the cause (first-order δ forcing of the theta-scheme, front interpolation, or something else; show the evidence, for example the order of the arrival time with a smoothed forcing or a different front detector in a scratch experiment); and how the reference starts its longitudinal modes (does it reproduce the Δt/2 shift that SI-0024 removes in the engine? state the measured offset). Do not change the longitudinal scheme in this follow-up; if a change is warranted, describe it in the report and the planner decides.
